Paper cards

Odd pages are the QR fronts, even pages the answers. Every QR points at {{ appUrl }}.

Get these two right

  • Scale 100%, actual size. Never “fit to page”, or the QR shrinks past the point a phone can read it.
  • Two-sided Flip on the long edge, the way a book opens, not end over end. The backs are already mirrored for that. A short-edge flip doesn’t just turn the answers upside down: it lands each one on the wrong card, and you’d only find out mid-game, after the paper is spent.
My printer can’t print both sides by itself

Print the odd pages first. In the print dialog set Pages to 1{{ paperCount() > 1 ? ', 3, 5…' : '' }}, or pick “Odd pages only” if your dialog offers it. Take that stack out, turn it over about the long edge (the way you turn a page in a book, not end over end), and put it back in the tray. Then print the even pages.

Which way up the paper goes back in differs per printer. Do one sheet first and hold it up to the light: the four corner marks on the front and back should sit exactly on top of each other. If they do not, turn the paper the other way and try once more.

3D tiles

'plate' : 'plates' }} of up to {{ plateCapacity }} tiles ({{ plateGrid }}), each sized for a 250 × 210 mm bed, so no plate is wider than your printer. {{ fileCount() > 1 ? 'They arrive together in a single .zip.' : 'It downloads as a single .stl.' }} The QR and its code stand proud on top; the artist, year and title are raised on the underside, mirrored so they read once you turn the tile over. Everything raised changes at one layer height, so a single filament colour-change gives dark marks on a light tile. The QR is small even at full tile size. Test-scan one before printing a whole deck.
